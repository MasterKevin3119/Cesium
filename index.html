Where We Are

This simulation is based on Kampung Asahan, used as a case study to represent a flood-prone community environment. The area provides a realistic setting for exploring how flooding can affect surrounding buildings, roads, and public spaces.

Flood Extent and Water Levels

To identify areas affected by different flood water levels, Climate Central–style information is used. This helps illustrate how rising water levels can impact land areas under various flood conditions.

3D Environment and Visualisation

An interactive 3D globe of the study area lets you visualise flood scenarios in an immersive way. Water levels and flood impacts are easier to observe and understand, making changes in conditions clearer.

Why This Matters

This simulation is designed to help you better understand your surroundings during flood events. By exploring different flood scenarios, you can see how conditions change and how decisions influence outcomes.

Make Better Decisions in a Real Crisis

By presenting flood scenarios visually and interactively, the simulation supports you in making more informed decisions before and during flood events.
