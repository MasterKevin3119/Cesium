Flood Awareness & Decision Making

Read flow direction, pick safe routes, and decide when no path is safe.

This mission teaches you to read flood flow on a map, choose safer escape routes at different water depths, and recognise when staying put is the better option.

  • Watch the flow arrows on the map panel to judge which direction floodwater is moving
  • Routes are highlighted — select every route you think is safe at the given depth before submitting
  • If all paths are underwater or unsafe, select "No safe route" instead of guessing
